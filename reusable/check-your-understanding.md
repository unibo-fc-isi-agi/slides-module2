# Check your understanding

<div style="font-size: 75%;">

* Answer in your own words, motivating your conclusions and using examples where useful.

* Questions asking whether something is possible or desirable require more than a yes/no answer.

* Implementation questions can be answered with an explanation or pseudocode unless executable code is explicitly requested.

* Labels identify the primary learning target:
    - **notions** (knowledge), **reasoning** (drawing conclusions), or **know-how** (applying knowledge).
    - **theoretical** questions are independent of particular technologies; **practical** questions concern their use.

* Practical answers should identify the technology used, without requiring memorisation of product-specific details (model names, prices, API signatures, versions, limits).
